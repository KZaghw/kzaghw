We maintain a Bayesian posterior over finite-dimensional mean embeddings of return distributions, chosen to capture higher-order moments while admitting Bellman-consistent propagation. This supports risk-aware value-of-perfect-information exploration that explicitly trades off epistemic uncertainty and risk.
